Analysis / germline.smk

Assign germline genes and measure sequence distance

Germline sequences are inherited antibody gene templates. Mature antibodies differ through rearrangement and mutation. This module asks how far each generated heavy chain is from its nearest reconstructed germline reference.

Rule: analysis_score_germline

Input: one run’s cohort.csv.

Output: metrics/germline.csv.

Script: score_germline.py.

Environment: analysis_anarci.yaml; four CPU threads.

ANARCI’s role

ANARCI numbers antibody variable-domain residues according to an antibody numbering scheme and assigns likely V (variable) and J (joining) germline genes. The scheme and allowed species come from configuration. Numbering aligns biologically corresponding positions even when sequence lengths differ.

Step by step

  1. Validate the generated amino-acid sequence.
  2. Run ANARCI with the configured numbering scheme.
  3. Record species, assigned V/J genes, identity values, and numbered heavy sequence.
  4. Construct the nearest available heavy-chain germline reference from the assignment.
  5. Compute Levenshtein distance: the minimum number of insertions, deletions, and substitutions needed to transform one sequence into the other.
  6. Also divide distance by an appropriate sequence length to produce a normalized value.
  7. Record a status when numbering or assignment is unavailable.

What Figure 4 means

A smaller distance indicates closer sequence similarity to the assigned germline reconstruction. It does not directly measure affinity maturation, immunogenicity, or safety. Comparisons should retain species/assignment context and should not treat failed assignments as a numeric distance.

Common mistakes

  • Calling raw edit distance a mutation count at aligned biological positions; insertions and deletions complicate that interpretation.
  • Comparing unnormalized distances across very different sequence lengths.
  • Assuming the assigned gene is certainly the historical ancestor rather than the closest database-supported assignment.
