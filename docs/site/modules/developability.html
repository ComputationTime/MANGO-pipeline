Analysis / developability.smk

Compute simple sequence developability descriptors

Developability asks whether a molecule has properties compatible with production, formulation, stability, and delivery. This module computes two inexpensive sequence descriptors; it is not a complete developability assessment.

Rule: analysis_biophysical

Input: one run’s deterministic cohort.csv.

Output: metrics/biophysical.csv.

Parameter: pH used for charge estimation, normally 7.4.

Script/environment: score_biophysical.py in analysis_core.yaml.

GRAVY hydropathy

GRAVY means Grand Average of Hydropathy. Each amino acid has a hydropathy value describing preference for water versus hydrophobic environments. The sequence score is the arithmetic mean across residues. More positive values generally indicate a more hydrophobic sequence. Hydropathy can influence solubility and aggregation, but context and three-dimensional exposure matter.

Estimated charge at a chosen pH

Acidic and basic groups gain or lose protons depending on pH. The script estimates net charge from sequence composition and standard dissociation behavior at the configured pH. It is an approximation: local structure can shift chemical properties.

Output provenance

The CSV retains embedder, run, target, design index, sequence, both metrics, pH, and status. Figure 5 plots distributions by antigen representation from this table.

TAP is deferred: the Therapeutic Antibody Profiler is not silently approximated here. GRAVY and charge are explicitly labeled simple sequence metrics and must not be called a full TAP result.

Interpretation limits

No universal “best” value exists independently of molecular context. These distributions are useful for spotting major shifts or extreme sequences, not for certifying a therapeutic candidate.
