Analysis / likelihood.smk

Collect likelihood results for Figure 1

This intentionally small module creates a named dependency boundary between model evaluation and plotting.

Rule: analysis_likelihood

Inputs: one eval.json per analysis embedder.

Outputs: none of its own; completion means all required evaluation files exist.

Computation: none. NLL and perplexity were computed by evaluate_model.

Why have a rule that does no computation?

It gives users and downstream rules a semantic target: “all likelihood inputs are ready.” It also keeps the scientific responsibility clear. The model module computes probabilities; the plot module only reads and visualizes them.

Metrics

Negative log-likelihood (NLL) penalizes low probability assigned to the observed next heavy-chain token. Lower NLL means better probability assignment. Perplexity is exp(mean NLL), also lower-is-better. JSON records split totals, token/example counts, and averages so aggregation is weighted correctly rather than averaging averages blindly.

Interpretation

Figure 1 compares train and cluster-held-out test. A low train value with much higher test value suggests overfitting. A representation that improves held-out NLL consistently is evidence for better conditional sequence modeling, but not direct evidence of binding or developability.
